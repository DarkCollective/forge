Accessibility

Forge is meant to be usable by everyone, with a keyboard, a screen reader, a magnifier or high contrast. Our goal is to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA. This page says what we aim for, what we know is missing, and how to tell us about a problem.

What we aim for

  • Everything can be done with the keyboard alone, in a logical focus order.
  • Focus is always visible, and is never removed.
  • Every control has a name, and states such as selected or invalid are announced.
  • Text and controls have enough contrast in every theme.
  • Layouts work at 200% zoom and at a width of 320 CSS pixels, without horizontal scrolling.
  • Motion is reduced when your system asks for it.

Themes

Forge has light, dark and high-contrast themes. High contrast uses black and white, thicker borders and a bright focus ring, and has no shadows. The system setting is followed by default, including the contrast preference of your operating system, and Windows forced colours are respected. Your choice is kept in your browser; see the privacy page.

Keyboard use

  • Alt with 1 to 4 moves focus to the operations, the recipe, the input and the output. Alt with 5 opens the console.
  • Every action that can be done by dragging, such as adding an operation or reordering steps, also has a keyboard route.
  • Esc closes a menu or dialog, and cancels a drag.

Known limitations

This list is short and will be refined as we test more.

  • Not every combination of browser and screen reader has been tested, so some announcements may not be what we intend.
  • Results of large inputs can arrive after a short delay. The app announces results in a separate status line rather than interrupting you.
  • Some help text assumes familiarity with encodings and ciphers, and not all of it has been reviewed for plain language yet.

Report a problem

Please tell us what you tried, what happened and what you expected, along with the page or feature, your browser, your operating system and any assistive technology you use. Write to david@darkcollective.com, or open an issue on GitHub.
