Privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This page is an inventory rather than a policy. It says what Forge does with what you enter, what is stored in your browser, and what the server records, and what each of those can expose.

What Forge does with your data

Forge runs entirely in your browser. The text, files, recipes and outputs you work with are processed by code this site has delivered to you. None of them are sent to any server. The site has no accounts, no forms to submit, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.

What this site loads

Every script, stylesheet, font, worker and data file comes from this site's own address. The app is only allowed to make requests to its own origin, and a content security policy enforces that in your browser. Your browser makes no requests to third parties.

Share links

A share link holds the recipe and, if you choose to include it, the input. Both are encoded in the part of the address after #. Browsers never send that part to a server, so it does not appear in the server logs.

That part is not secret. Anyone you give the link to can read the recipe and the input, including any key or password parameters, which travel in the link in clear. Only share links with people you are content to show them to.

Server logs

The site is served by Amazon CloudFront. Its access logs record, for each request, the IP address, the time, the path requested, the user agent and the referrer. They never include the part of the address after #. The logs are kept for 90 days, then deleted. They are used only to run the site and to find faults in it.

What is stored in your browser

Forge keeps the following in your browser's own storage. None of it leaves your browser. Forge writes each item only when you change the setting or use the feature it belongs to.

Local storage in this browser
Name What it holds
forge.layout.v1 in local storage The sizes of the panes, and which panes are collapsed.
forge.theme in local storage Your theme choice: system, light, dark or high contrast.
IndexedDB database forge in this browser
Store What it holds
recipes Recipes you save, and the input stored with a recipe if you chose to include it.
kv Your favourite operations, your options (such as auto-bake), and the words you define yourself.

How to delete it

  • To remove one saved recipe or one of your own words, delete it inside Forge.
  • To remove everything, clear the site data for forge.darkcollective.com in your browser's settings. That deletes all of the above. Saved recipes, favourites and your words are lost, and the layout and theme return to their defaults.
