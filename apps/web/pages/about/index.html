Open Forge

About Forge

Forge is a browser-only toolkit for codes, ciphers and data encodings. You build a recipe from operations, give it an input, and read the output. Everything runs in your browser.

What it does

  • Search or browse a catalogue of operations, grouped by category, with favourites for the ones you use most.
  • Drag operations into a recipe, or add them from the keyboard, set their parameters, and watch the output change as you edit.
  • Save recipes and share them as a link. A share link can also carry the input, so read the privacy page before sending one.

How it is built

Each operation is a word in FL, a small Forth-like language. Operations are written in FL or backed by small TypeScript primitives. A recipe is also a word, so a recipe can be named, saved and reused as an operation of its own. You can see the FL behind any recipe, and define new words in the console.

Where it comes from

Forge is inspired by CyberChef, the tool published by GCHQ, and uses its range of capabilities as a reference. Forge is an independent implementation. Its code, operation names, parameters and recipe format are its own. Forge is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by GCHQ or the CyberChef project.

Open source

Forge is free software, released under the Apache License 2.0. The source, the issue tracker and the full history are on GitHub.
